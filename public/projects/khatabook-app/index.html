Project Overview

Khatabook App helps users maintain clear transaction records and business account visibility. The platform focuses on simple workflows, accurate ledger tracking, and a responsive interface that supports day-to-day operational management.

  • Transaction and balance tracking
  • Historical record management
  • Business finance oversight
  • Clean, accessible web experience
