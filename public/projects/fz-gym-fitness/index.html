Project Overview

FZ Gym Fitness is a web-based fitness and management platform that centralizes memberships, workout planning, service visibility, and operational reporting. It is built for modern gyms that want a streamlined digital experience for both administrators and clients.

  • Membership plans and user onboarding flow
  • Workout and fitness service management
  • Administrative dashboards and reporting
  • Responsive, user-friendly interface
